What Is the MSCI World Index?

A straightforward explanation of one of the world's most widely followed developed-market equity indexes.

A Global Developed-Market Index

The MSCI World Index captures large and mid-cap companies across developed markets. MSCI states that the index covers approximately 85% of the free-float-adjusted market capitalisation in each country.

The index is not itself an investment fund. Investors can obtain exposure through funds and exchange-traded products designed to track the index.

Why Investors Study It

Instead of relying on the performance of one company or one country, the MSCI World Index provides a broad way of measuring developed-market equity performance.

However, diversification does not remove investment risk. The index has experienced substantial declines during previous market downturns.

Global Diversification

MSCI World provides exposure to developed-market equity markets rather than concentrating on a single country.

🌎

23 Developed Markets

The MSCI World Index covers large and mid-cap companies across 23 developed markets.

🏢

Large & Mid Cap

The index focuses on large- and mid-cap companies and aims to represent approximately 85% of each country's free-float-adjusted market capitalisation.

📊

Market-Cap Weighted

Larger companies generally have greater index weights, which means the index can become concentrated in its largest companies.

The Risks You Need to Understand

A professional investment analysis should explain both potential returns and potential losses.

Market Risk

Share prices can fall substantially. A diversified global index can still experience significant declines.

Currency Risk

A UK investor's actual return can be affected by movements between sterling and the currencies of the underlying investments.

Concentration Risk

Although the index contains many companies, the largest companies can account for meaningful portions of the total index.

Fund Tracking Risk

An ETF or fund tracking MSCI World may not exactly match the index because of fees, taxes, transaction costs and tracking differences.

What is the MSCI World Index?

The MSCI World Index is an equity index covering large and mid-cap companies across developed markets. MSCI states that it covers approximately 85% of free-float-adjusted market capitalisation within each country.

Is MSCI World an investment fund?

No. MSCI World is an index. Investors can obtain exposure through funds and ETFs that track the index.

How many companies are in MSCI World?

MSCI reported 1,282 constituents as of 31 July 2026. The number can change as companies enter or leave the index.
