What is the MSCI World Index?

The MSCI World Index is a widely followed global equity index designed to represent large and mid-cap companies across developed markets.

Because it provides exposure across multiple developed economies and sectors, the index is frequently used by investors when researching global equity diversification.

Why do investors research the MSCI World Index?

Understanding the structure and characteristics of the MSCI World Index can help investors assess how global developed-market equities may fit within a broader investment strategy.

  • Exposure to companies across multiple developed markets.
  • Diversification across different industries and sectors.
  • Understanding geographical and market exposure.
  • Researching historical index performance.
  • Considering risk, volatility and long-term investment objectives.

MSCI World Index Research

Investors researching the MSCI World Index may wish to consider more than simply historical returns. Market concentration, geographical exposure, sector weightings, valuation, economic conditions and currency movements can all be relevant when assessing global equity investments.

The MSCI World Index should therefore be considered as part of wider investment research rather than viewed in isolation.

Is the MSCI World Index suitable for everyone?

No investment is suitable for everyone. The appropriate level of investment risk depends on individual circumstances, objectives, time horizon and tolerance for losses.

Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. The value of investments can fall as well as rise and investors may receive back less than they originally invested.
